Four rules

1 · Something goes up every session. Weight, reps, depth, rung, or less help. Nothing moved twice in a row? Change the exercise. Not "try harder."

2 · The last 2 reps are hard. Could've done 5 more? That was a warm-up. First block is the exception — it stops while you're still fast and clean.

3 · Chase two things, just do the rest. Till Nov 11: the muscle-up and the ladder. After: jumps and the RDL.

4 · Vest on once, stays on. Weight goes on the page, not in your head.
